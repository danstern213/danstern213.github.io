About Me

I learned how to code because I wanted to build self-driving cars. I never got very far there, but it propelled me into the world of software. I currently lead Customer Success at a Healthtech startup called Adonis.

I previously co-founded and led Product for a sports tech company that was acquired by Beckett. Before that I worked at Palantir, doing everything from demoing and selling supply chain readiness workflows, writing PySpark, and tracking spend for the Army.
